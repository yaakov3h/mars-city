# MARS CITY future phases

Confirmed direction, September 30, 2026:
- Underground is the main circulation layer. Building interiors and floors are the second layer.
- Glazed sections in selected plazas only. Preserve above-ground architecture and natural crater reserves.
- Fully open structures do not fit the Martian environment: enclose pavilions with glazed window walls between the exposed frames.
- Walking first. Reserve road space for future vehicles; driving is not part of this pilot.

Future idea preserved, not implemented or authorized for immediate construction:

Pilot scope: one street joining founding and creative hexes, one glass-roof section, one vertical access lift and one experimental atelier room. Multi-floor interiors, a full road network, driving, NPC traffic, flight systems and multiplayer remain future work.

- Underground areas with seating, a garden, cafes, restaurants and shops, added slowly over time.
- Phase 1: connect the Layers Junction lift shaft to the pilot network. Plan properly before building; quality over speed.
